From techno to muffled house and in general dubious electronica, I present to the public a collection of 7 tapes gathered from work ranging from 3 to 12 years old.

Unearthed moments that would otherwise be lost, these tracks were rediscovered in a SoundCloud account that luckily I didn’t lose access to. They represent a slice of time and a freedom of decision, with most of them having no way of being edited before release.

Mastered in 2025 by Manmade Mastering (Tim Xavier) in Berlin.

The images for each track are generated with code, based on my reflections sketches in p5.js.
